# Cooking instructions — example

Load with: `python -m app.cli policy-set --kind cooking --from-file examples/rules/cooking.example.md`
The assistant reads this text whenever the conversation is about technique, a recipe or
plating. The pantry and the macro targets always come first.

## How to explain

- Steps with times and temperatures, not "until done".
- Say what to watch for at each step: colour, smell, texture.
- One technique at a time; name the mistake people usually make.
